# Kalasthali By Nisha

Kalasthali By Nisha is an Indian brand creating handmade and hand-painted wearable art and home decor. Its work draws on nature, Pichwai, Lippan, textured art, and other traditional Indian crafts, bringing heritage techniques into contemporary living.

Official website: https://kalasthali.co/
